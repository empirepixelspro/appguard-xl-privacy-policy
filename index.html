AppGuard XL Android

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AppGuard XL (developed by Empire Pixels Pro) handles information when you use our Android application. AppGuard XL has no backend server — all of your backups, PINs, notes and scan results stay safely on your device. By using the app, you agree to the use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. 100% Local Storage — No Cloud

AppGuard XL is built on a local-first, zero-server principle. Every piece of user data is stored 100% locally on your Android device. Empire Pixels Pro does not operate cloud storage, and no personal data is ever transmitted to our servers — we do not collect, upload, read, share or sell your APK backups, installed app lists, PINs or passwords, intruder photos, location records, or private vault notes. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all locally stored records are permanently deleted from your device.

2. Information the App Accesses

The app requests only the permissions required for its core security and backup features, and everything below is processed on your device:

  • Installed app list (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): used locally to audit security risks, compute SHA-256 checksums, back up selected APKs, and choose apps for the Per-App Lock.
  • APK backup files: stored in the app's private local storage for restore, version history and offline sharing — never uploaded anywhere.
  • PINs, passwords and biometrics: held in local preferences and Android Keystore / BiometricPrompt; fingerprint and face data are handled entirely by the Android OS.
  • Camera and approximate location (optional): requested only if you enable the Intruder Alert, which captures a front-camera photo, coarse location and timestamp after 3+ failed unlock attempts. There is no precise or background location access.
  • Usage access and overlay (optional): required only if you switch on the real-time Per-App Lock Shield; you can turn both off at any time from the Banking-Safe control on the dashboard.
  • Private Mini-Vault notes: saved through the Android Photo Picker and local storage — no broad storage permissions are used.
  • What we do NOT collect: we never collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, installed app inventories, or unique device identifiers with any server we operate.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To list, audit and back up the apps you select, entirely on-device.
  • To lock the apps you choose with a PIN, password or biometric unlock.
  • To run threat, permission and SHA-256 scans locally on your phone.
  • To clean storage and show real device usage statistics.
  • To process one-time purchases securely through Google Play Billing (Pro Lifetime) — payment details are never seen or stored by us.

4. Target Audience & Age Appropriateness

AppGuard XL is intended for a general audience of 13 years and older. The application provides device-security and backup tools suitable for everyday users and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app is not directed at children under 13.

5. Advertising

AppGuard XL may display Google AdMob advertisements on the free tier. The Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may process device and advertising identifiers and app interactions (ad views, clicks and general app activity), plus diagnostic data, to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings. Ads are removed permanently when Pro Lifetime is activated. No other data is shared with advertisers.

6. Third-Party SDKs & Data Types

The only third-party software development kits (SDKs) embedded in AppGuard XL are the two Google SDKs listed below. This list matches the data disclosures declared in Google Play's Data safety form:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) — collects device or other IDs (including the advertising ID), app interactions and diagnostics. Shared with Google for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Subject to Google's privacy policy and Google Play policies.
  • Google Play Billing — collects purchase history (your one-time Pro Lifetime purchase) for app functionality, so the app can verify your entitlement. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play and are never seen or stored by Empire Pixels Pro.
  • Optional crash and feedback reports — only if you tap "Send report", the app forwards crash logs and the message you typed to our support inbox. Nothing is sent automatically, and no report is created if you do not tap the button.
  • Processed only on your device — photos (intruder selfie) and approximate location are captured only when you enable the Intruder Alert, are stored in the app's private local storage, and are never uploaded to any server. They are not shared with the SDKs above.
  • Not collected: APK backups, installed app lists, PINs and passwords, private vault notes and scan results never leave your device.

No other SDK, analytics provider, advertising network or data broker is integrated into the application.

7. Data Security & Deletion

All app data (APK backups, lock settings, scan results, intruder logs and vault notes) is stored locally on your device. The app has no backend of its own, so there is no remote copy to breach.

Data Deletion: you retain full control. Erase everything from within the app's settings, or simply uninstall AppGuard XL — all local records are removed permanently. To ask about any support records, contact us by email.
